Beam

Element spacing λ/2: channel n gets φn = −180°·n·sin θ. Four elements set a beam about 26° wide; the 20° beam in the link budget comes from the full sub-array.

MIMO · 4 TX × 4 RX (TDM)

vr − mean across the beam

In MIMO mode the TX channels fire in turn (colors) and the angle is formed digitally from the 16 virtual elements. The fan shows each angle bin, colored by how much faster (red) or slower (blue) the rain approaches than the beam average. The left/right tilt of that pattern is the horizontal wind, shown as arrows: solid = retrieved, grey = truth.
